WHO YOU ARE TALKING TO02
THE 30 SECOND VERSION

What VIIBE actually is

Scouts inside a venue confirm what the room feels like right now, in three seconds. Readings decay in minutes, because energy does. The result is a live city map where every gathering glows by how alive it is: white hot for packed, grey for dead.

Consumers stop guessing where to go. Venues pay for the truth about their own rooms. Machines query the same feed, which is how VIIBE becomes the layer assistants answer "where's good tonight" from.

Nightlife in Lagos is market one, not the identity. The engine does not know what a nightclub is. Concerts, festivals and street events are configuration, not rebuilds.

TIER 4 · THE EXIT ASSETS

What an acquirer buys

  • The assistant layer. When someone asks Siri, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini "where's good tonight", the answer has to come from somewhere. The agent API and MCP server are live today.
  • Movement and taste data. Who goes where, with whom, at what hour, across a whole city.
  • Ride demand. Uber and Bolt want to know where crowds will be at 2am before they are.
  • The category position. Nobody owns live human energy data in Africa. That is the thing being bought.
FIX THIS BEFORE SELLING ANYTHING
PAID BOOST CURRENTLY INFLATES THE SCORE

In the scoring engine, a purchased Pulse Drop adds directly to the public energy score. A venue can pay to look hotter than it is. That is the one thing that, if noticed, kills the venue and brand business at once: nobody licenses crowd data that can be bought.

Fix: paid money buys placement, badges, position. Never the number.
