HOW IT READS

A sensor network made of people

Three stages between a room's energy and your map. Each one exists to keep the signal honest.

STAGE 1 — SENSE

Scouts confirm the room

A 3-second vibe check — energy, capacity, the gate — accepted only from inside the venue's geofence. You can't rate a room you're not in.

STAGE 2 — WEIGH

Reputation is staked

Every signal carries its scout's credibility weight, earned by track record. Scouts stake clout on predictions. Being right compounds; noise fades.

STAGE 3 — DECAY

The truth expires

Energy readings decay in minutes, like the energy itself. Peak status must be sustained and corroborated — a burst of hype reads as a burst, not a peak.
