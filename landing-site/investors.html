THE MARKET STRUCTURE

An intel market, not a listings app

  • Scouts (supply) submit geofenced 3-second vibe checks; every signal carries a credibility weight earned by track record. Scouts stake reputation on predictions and earn on accuracy.
  • Venues (first buyers) pay for the truth about their own rooms: live watcher counts, verified-arrival attribution, and a weekly Lift Report that prices their promo spend in naira.
  • Agents & assistants (the endgame) buy the aggregate feed. When someone asks Siri, ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini "where is alive tonight?", the answer has to come from somewhere — no incumbent holds this signal. The agent-distribution API is live in production today; assistant integrations are the roadmap, and the acquirer thesis.

SCENE-AGNOSTIC

Rating taxonomies and the decay engine don't know what a nightclub is. Concerts, festivals, street events are configuration, not rebuilds.

WHY NIGHTLIFE, WHY LAGOS

Every horizontal platform won a vertical first. Nightlife is the beachhead because its energy decays fastest (minutes — where live intel is worth the most), a paying buyer exists tonight (venues spend heavily on promotion with zero attribution), and it's where the scout habit forms. Lagos is one of the world's great nightlife cities with the weakest incumbent data coverage.
